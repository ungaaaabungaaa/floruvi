## Price evidence

Every new price is a retail benchmark multiplied by 1.40, rounded to the nearest paise. Existing owner prices are preserved.

[Supple Agro watercress](https://microgreens.co.in/products/watercress) lists ₹169 per 100 g. Nutriofarms WooCommerce variant data confirmed 50 g packs: beet, fenugreek and coriander at ₹149; garden cress at ₹179. All four variants were in stock when read.

[NayaGreens Bengaluru](https://nayagreens.com/microgreens-live/) lists live fenugreek and red amaranth trays at ₹130. Floruvi uses that tray-category benchmark for its six trays. Dimensions and harvest weights are not matched; these are comparable-based launch prices. No DIY kit or empty tray price was used.

For the remaining uncommon crops, the sheet marks category comparators explicitly. Tatsoi uses bok choy; mizuna uses arugula; lemon balm and chervil use fresh tarragon; kohlrabi microgreens use red cabbage microgreens; beefsteak tomatoes use Roma tomatoes. Individual flower varieties use the [Supple Agro assorted edible flower 40 g pack](https://microgreens.co.in/products/assorted-mix) at ₹250 as a category comparator. These are commercial launch prices, not exact retail quotes for those varieties.

See [all prices and sources](pricing-benchmarks.csv). The coverage test prevents a new seed product without a price. Missing or invalid live data still cannot produce a complete basket total.
